# Botium Toys Security Audit

## Project Overview

This project presents a security controls and compliance assessment of **Botium Toys**, a fictional company used as a case study in the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate.

The objective of the audit was to review the organization's current security controls, identify potential risks and compliance gaps, and recommend measures to improve its overall security posture.

The original risk assessment assigned Botium Toys a **risk score of 8/10**, mainly due to missing security controls and incomplete adherence to compliance best practices.

---

## Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

- Evaluate existing administrative, technical, and physical security controls
- Identify gaps that could affect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data
- Assess risks associated with sensitive customer and payment data
- Recommend security controls and improvements to reduce risk

---

## Security Controls Assessment

The assessment identified several controls already implemented by Botium Toys, including:

- Firewall
- Antivirus software
- Password policy
- Physical locks
- CCTV surveillance
- Fire detection and prevention systems
- Manual monitoring and maintenance of legacy systems
- Controls supporting data integrity and availability

However, several important security controls were missing or insufficient, including:

- Principle of least privilege
- Separation of duties
- Encryption
- Intrusion Detection System (IDS)
- Disaster recovery plans
- Backups of critical data
- Centralized password management
- Regular maintenance schedules for legacy systems

---

## Compliance Assessment

### PCI DSS

The assessment identified significant gaps related to the protection of payment card information.

Key issues included:

- Excessive employee access to cardholder data
- Lack of encryption for credit card information
- Inadequate password management
- Payment data not being handled in a sufficiently secure environment

---

### GDPR

Some GDPR-related practices were already implemented, including:

- A process to notify E.U. customers within 72 hours of a data breach
- Privacy policies, procedures, and processes for documenting and maintaining data

However, improvements were needed in:

- Protecting customers' personal data
- Restricting access to PII/SPII
- Asset and data classification
- Asset inventory management

---

### SOC

The assessment found that Botium Toys had controls supporting:

- Data integrity
- Data availability

However, weaknesses remained in:

- User access controls
- Confidentiality of PII/SPII

---

## Key Recommendations

Based on the assessment, the following improvements were recommended:

- Implement encryption to protect sensitive and payment data
- Apply the principle of least privilege
- Implement separation of duties
- Develop disaster recovery plans
- Perform regular backups of critical data
- Implement an Intrusion Detection System (IDS)
- Strengthen password complexity requirements
- Adopt a centralized password management system
- Establish a regular monitoring and maintenance schedule for legacy systems
- Create and maintain an asset inventory
- Classify assets according to their sensitivity and potential business impact

These measures would help reduce risks and strengthen the **confidentiality, integrity, and availability** of Botium Toys' information.
